# StudWorx

StudWorx was born out of a simple frustration: no existing LEGO® CAD
application offered the completeness, flexibility and workflow I was
looking for. Rather than settle for compromises or work around the
limitations of existing tools, I decided to take matters into my own
hands and build my own.

Based on LeoCAD and developed into an independent, fully featured
LDraw-based modeling application, StudWorx brings together the tools and
capabilities needed to design, build and explore virtual LEGO creations.
With an ongoing focus on usability, functionality and workflow
refinement, the project is driven by a simple idea: if the tool you need
doesn't exist, create it.

**BUILD. DESIGN. CREATE.**
